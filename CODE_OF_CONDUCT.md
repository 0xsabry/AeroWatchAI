# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity, and
to keep discussion focused on the work: evidence, measurements, and
engineering quality.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and claims, not
  people.
- Back assertions with evidence — benchmarks, tests, citations.
- Accept correction gracefully; honest mistakes, promptly fixed,
  are valued here.
- Keep safety and licensing discussions factual and in scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Knowingly false technical claims, fabricated results, or pressure
  to bypass the project's safety or licensing rules.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or block
participants who violate this code. Report problems by opening an
issue marked "conduct" or contacting the maintainer through the
repository owner's GitHub profile.
